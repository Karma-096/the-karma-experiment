      FAQ

      Is this a charity?

      No. It is a private creative social experiment. Contributions go to a private individual.

      Will a contribution improve my karma?

      No such outcome is promised or measurable here. “Karma” is a metaphor for voluntary kindness.

      Are donations anonymous?

      Public names are optional. Blockchain transfers are public, and services may require identity verification.

      How is the ranking calculated?

      Countries are ordered by total verified contributions, with amounts hidden. Country data is volunteered and cannot be independently inferred from a wallet address.
